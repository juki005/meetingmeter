5. V1.1 DESIGN ADDITIONS
==================================================

TECH / AG must account for the following approved Product v1.1
capabilities as represented in the proposed Design:

MANUAL ENTRY
- required date;
- required meeting duration;
- participants;
- individual participation duration;
- optional time;
- optional manual-entry reason;
- MANUAL ENTRY provenance;
- 0 <= participation <= meeting duration;
- zero duration valid;
- editing and recalculation;
- no fabricated live events;
- History and Receipt representation.

PREPARED
- saved future meeting;
- no measurement before Start;
- Edit;
- Reschedule;
- Delete;
- Start.

MISSED / NOT HELD
- History representation;
- distinct from ENDED;
- not a zero-cost completed meeting;
- Product-supported follow-up actions.

RECURRING
- visible tag/indicator;
- duplication;
- independent duplicated meeting records;
- no recurrence engine/calendar synchronization.

EXTERNAL COSTS
- multiple items;
- name/description;
- amount;
- EUR (€);
- zero allowed;
- negative prohibited;
- participant/external separation;
- combined total formula.

COST LIBRARY
- name;
- default amount;
- EUR (€);
- copy default into meeting;
- per-meeting override;
- library default remains independent.

POST-END EXTERNAL COST EDITING
- participant measurement remains locked;
- external costs can be edited;
- current estimated total may change;
- Last Updated changes;
- Receipt/History update;
- meeting does not restart.
