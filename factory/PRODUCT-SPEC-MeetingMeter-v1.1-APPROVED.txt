==================================================
1. PRODUCT OBJECTIVE
==================================================

PROBLEM
Meetings consume the time of multiple people, but their cumulative
estimated cost is often not visible while the meeting is taking place.

OBJECTIVE
Enable the person who creates and starts a MeetingMeter session to
see the estimated cost of the meeting in real time, based on the
people currently included in measurement.

CORE OUTCOME
The organizer can configure a meeting, include people, start the
Meter, see the estimated cost accumulate according to individual
measured participation time, end the meeting, and review/export the
final result.

==================================================
2. PRODUCT CHARACTER
==================================================

MeetingMeter is not intended to be an accounting, payroll, billing,
or payment system.

It is an informative tool that presents the estimated cost of a
meeting in a cool, light, and mildly humorous/sarcastic way.

Core concept:
"How much is this meeting costing us?"

Humor is directed at the situation and the time/resource consumed by
the meeting, not at individual users.

Example tone direction:
"Meeting cost: EUR 184.20"
"This could have been an email."

The product tone must not obscure the actual numerical result.

==================================================
3. USERS AND ROLES
==================================================

3.1 MEETING ORGANIZER / CREATOR

The primary user is the person who creates and starts the MeetingMeter
session.

The organizer:
- creates the meeting;
- adds people;
- adds guests;
- selects who is currently being measured;
- starts the Meter;
- monitors cost;
- pauses participants;
- resumes participants;
- marks participants as having left;
- ends the meeting;
- reviews meeting history;
- exports the result.

The meeting belongs to its creator.

3.2 REGISTERED USER

A registered user has a MeetingMeter account and a personal profile.

The personal profile contains at minimum:
- name;
- job title;
- hourly rate.

The user is responsible for their own hourly rate.

The user's profile is personal and protected.

3.3 INTERNAL PERSON

An internal person is a person available to the organizer as part of
the organizer's people/organization context.

The organizer can create and add people for use in meetings.

3.4 GUEST / EXTERNAL PERSON

A Guest is a person who participates in a meeting but does not have a
MeetingMeter account and is not necessarily part of the organizer's
organization.

Typical example:
An agency has a meeting with a client who is external to the agency.

A Guest may have known or unknown information.

Known Guest information may include:
- name;
- role;
- organization;
- hourly rate.

A Guest does not need a MeetingMeter account.

==================================================
4. PERSONAL PROFILE
==================================================

A registered user has a personal profile containing:
- Name
- Job title
- Hourly rate

The hourly rate is the value MeetingMeter uses for that person's
meeting-cost calculation.

The user is responsible for maintaining their own hourly rate.

The personal profile is protected.

The organizer may see the hourly rate of participants in the
organizer's meeting.

==================================================
5. PEOPLE MODEL
==================================================

The person who runs MeetingMeter creates and manages the people
available for their meetings.

The organizer can maintain people and then select them when
configuring a meeting.

The model includes:
- internal people;
- external Guests.

A registered internal person may have their own MeetingMeter profile.

A Guest does not need an account.

For a Guest whose cost is known, the organizer may provide the
relevant cost information.

For a Guest whose cost is not known, the cost remains explicitly
unknown.

Unknown cost must not be treated as zero.

==================================================
6. MEETING MODEL
==================================================

A meeting is a session created by an organizer.

In v1.1, a meeting may exist in one of the following product states:
- PREPARED;
- LIVE;
- ENDED;
- MISSED / NOT HELD (History representation for a prepared meeting
  whose planned date/time passed without Start);
- MANUAL ENTRY (provenance/type for a reconstructed historical meeting).

A meeting contains:
- creator/owner;
- roster;
- participant measurement states where applicable;
- individual measured participation time;
- individual estimated cost where known;
- external cost items where applicable;
- participant cost total;
- external cost total;
- total estimated meeting cost;
- final/result record;
- history record;
- measurement type/provenance where applicable.

The creator owns the meeting and its history.

Other users do not automatically have access to the creator's
meeting history.

UNCHANGED FROM v1.0:
The meeting remains owned by its creator and retains the existing
participant, cost, result, history, and privacy concepts.

NEW IN v1.1:
PREPARED and MISSED / NOT HELD are introduced for prepared-meeting
planning/history behavior. MANUAL ENTRY is introduced as explicit
provenance. External cost items and separate cost totals are introduced.

==================================================

7. MEETING ROSTER
==================================================

The organizer can add people to the meeting roster.

The roster may contain:
- internal people;
- Guests.

The organizer can select individual people or use Select All.

The organizer does not need to obtain confirmation from each
participant before measuring them.

Participants do not need to be active MeetingMeter users.

==================================================
8. PARTICIPANT MEASUREMENT LIFECYCLE
==================================================

Each participant has an individual measurement state.

ACTIVE
The participant is currently included in measurement. Their measured
participation time increases.

PAUSED
The participant is temporarily excluded from measurement. Their
measured participation time and cost stop increasing. The participant
remains part of the meeting and can be resumed.

RESUMED
A paused participant can be activated again. Measurement continues
from the point of resumption.

LEFT
When a participant leaves the meeting, their measurement for that
meeting ends.

LEFT is distinct from PAUSED.

Example:
Temporary absence: PAUSE -> RESUME
Participant leaves the meeting: LEAVE

==================================================
8.1 PREPARED MEETING LIFECYCLE
==================================================

NEW IN v1.1

A PREPARED meeting is a persistent, saved meeting that has been
configured but has not yet been started.

PREPARED:
- has planned/configured information;
- does not run a timer;
- does not accumulate meeting cost;
- may be edited before Start;
- may be rescheduled before Start;
- may be deleted before Start.

The normal live lifecycle is:

PREPARED -> LIVE -> ENDED

If the planned date/time passes without Start, the meeting may be
represented in History as MISSED / NOT HELD.

A MISSED / NOT HELD meeting:
- does not accumulate cost;
- is not treated as a completed live meeting with zero cost;
- may be retained in History;
- may be deleted;
- may be used as the basis for Manual Entry if the meeting actually
  occurred.

Cancellation is not a separate product state required by v1.1.

Rescheduling updates the existing prepared meeting and records a
visible Last Updated or equivalent change indication.

RECURRING

NEW IN v1.1

A meeting may have a RECURRING indicator/tag.

Recurring behavior is limited to:
- marking a meeting as recurring;
- duplicating it into another meeting for easier preparation.

A duplicated occurrence is an independent meeting record and can be
edited independently.

Recurring is not an automatic recurrence/calendar scheduling engine.
No calendar event is automatically created or synchronized by this
capability.

==================================================

9. MEASUREMENT START
==================================================

Measurement begins only after the organizer explicitly starts the
Meter.

Creating, configuring, or saving a PREPARED meeting does not by itself start
measurement.

For a PREPARED meeting, Start is the transition from PREPARED to LIVE.

When Start is activated:
- the meeting becomes active;
- active selected participants begin accumulating measured time;
- live cost begins accumulating according to the calculation model.

==================================================
10. CALCULATION MODEL
==================================================

Individual participant cost =
hourly rate x measured participation time

Participant Cost Total =
sum of individual participant costs

External Cost Total =
sum of external cost items

Total Estimated Meeting Cost =
Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total

Meeting duration and individual measured participation time are
therefore not necessarily the same.

A participant may be:
- active for the whole meeting;
- paused for part of the meeting;
- resumed later;
- marked as having left before the meeting ends.

Only measured active participation time contributes to that person's
cost.

KNOWN COST
Participants with known hourly rates contribute to the calculated
participant cost.

UNKNOWN COST
A participant whose hourly cost is unknown is not treated as having
zero cost.

The meeting may still be completed.

The final result must make incomplete cost information visible, for
example:
Meeting cost: EUR 247.80
+ 1 participant with unknown cost

==================================================
10.1 EXTERNAL COSTS
==================================================

NEW IN v1.1

A meeting may contain zero or more external cost items.

Each external cost item contains:
- description/name;
- amount;
- meeting currency.

Currency for MeetingMeter v1.1 is EUR (€).

Zero amount is allowed.

Negative amounts are not allowed.

Multiple external cost items are allowed.

Participant costs and external costs are displayed separately, with
the combined Total Estimated Meeting Cost calculated as:

Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total.

External cost items may be edited after the meeting has ended.

Each approved external-cost edit recalculates the Total Estimated
Meeting Cost.

Post-finalization external-cost edits:
- do not restart the meeting;
- do not change participant measurement history;
- do not alter recorded live participant measurement;
- may change the current estimated total;
- update the receipt/history result;
- update Last Updated information;
- retain the result as ESTIMATED.

==================================================
10.2 COST LIBRARY
==================================================

NEW IN v1.1

MeetingMeter includes a Cost Library for reusable external cost
items.

A Cost Library item contains at minimum:
- name;
- default amount;
- currency.

A user may select a Cost Library item when adding an external cost to
a meeting.

The selected default amount is copied into the meeting's external
cost item.

The applied meeting price may be overridden for that specific
meeting.

A per-meeting override:
- does not modify the Cost Library default;
- is stored independently on the meeting;
- remains the applied historical price even if the Cost Library
  default is changed later.

==================================================

11. LIVE METER
==================================================

During an active meeting, the organizer can see at minimum:
- active measurement state;
- elapsed meeting time;
- current total estimated cost;
- participant measurement states;
- current participant cost total;
- current external cost total where external costs have been added;
- current total estimated meeting cost.

The accumulated cost changes according to currently active
participants and their measured participation times.

Individual participant measurement is reflected in the total.

==================================================
12. MEETING END
==================================================

The organizer explicitly ends the meeting.

When the meeting ends:
- active measurement stops;
- active participant timers stop;
- the live participant measurement result is finalized/locked;
- the meeting result is stored in meeting history;
- the meeting no longer accumulates participant measurement over time.

UNCHANGED FROM v1.0:
A completed meeting does not continue accumulating participant cost
because time continues to pass.

SUPERSEDED BY v1.1:
The v1.0 rule that the entire final result is permanently fixed after
End is no longer universally applicable.

NEW IN v1.1:
After End, external cost items may be edited if this capability is
approved. Such edits recalculate the Total Estimated Meeting Cost and
update the stored receipt/history result.

The live participant measurement itself remains locked after End.

The result remains explicitly ESTIMATED.

==================================================

13. FINAL RECEIPT
==================================================

The final receipt is the visual final record of the estimated meeting
cost.

It is not an official financial document, payroll record, or
accounting document.

The final receipt contains enough information to understand the
result, including at minimum:
- meeting;
- creator;
- date/time where known;
- duration;
- participants;
- measured/reconstructed participation times where applicable;
- relevant participant cost information;
- external cost items where applicable;
- participant cost total;
- external cost total;
- final/current total estimated cost;
- measurement type/provenance where applicable;
- Last Updated where applicable.

For Manual Entry meetings, the receipt identifies the meeting as
Manual Entry and makes clear that the result was reconstructed rather
than produced by live measurement.

The final/current result is exportable.

The result remains explicitly ESTIMATED and is not an official
financial, payroll, or accounting amount.

==================================================

14. EXPORT
==================================================

The final meeting result can be exported in:
- PDF;
- Image;
- CSV.

==================================================
15. MEETING HISTORY
==================================================

Completed meetings are retained in history.

NEW IN v1.1:
History may also contain:
- MANUAL ENTRY meetings;
- MISSED / NOT HELD prepared meetings.

The creator can review their meetings and their results.

History identifies relevant provenance/state, including where
applicable:
- MANUAL ENTRY;
- MISSED / NOT HELD;
- RECURRING indicator;
- Last Updated.

For external-cost edits after finalization, History reflects the
current recalculated estimated total and Last Updated information.

Manual Entry may include the user-entered manual-entry reason in
History/detail visibility.

Meeting history is private.

A user cannot automatically access another user's private meeting
history.

==================================================

16. AUTHENTICATION AND PRIVACY
==================================================

The MVP uses a login/account model.

Authentication and access protection are required because MeetingMeter
contains potentially sensitive information including:
- personal hourly rates;
- meeting history;
- meeting costs;
- organizational relationships.

Default product rule:
A user can access their own data and meetings they own.

Another user's private meeting data is not automatically available.

The exact technical implementation of authentication and authorization
belongs to downstream technical definition.

A Manual Entry meeting can be edited and its estimated result

AC-35 EXTERNAL COSTS
A meeting can contain multiple external cost items with description
and amount.

External cost amount may be zero but cannot be negative.

The system calculates External Cost Total separately and combines it
with Participant Cost Total into Total Estimated Meeting Cost.

A meeting retains its applied external-cost amount independently of
later Cost Library changes.

AC-41 POST-FINALIZATION EDIT
External cost items can be edited after End.

AC-42 POST-FINALIZATION RECALCULATION

Estimated Meeting Cost without restarting the meeting or changing
locked participant measurement.

AC-43 LAST UPDATED
Post-finalization external-cost editing updates Last Updated
information.

AC-44 ESTIMATE FRAMING
The current result remains clearly identified as ESTIMATED after
external-cost edits.

==================================================

v1.0 BR-07 / AC-16 is superseded as an absolute rule that the entire
final result can never change after End.

- live participant measurement is locked after End;
- external-cost edits may change the current Total Estimated Meeting
  Cost after End;
- the result remains ESTIMATED.

No other approved v1.0 requirement is intentionally removed by this
proposal.
