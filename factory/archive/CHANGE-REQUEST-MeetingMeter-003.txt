CHANGE-REQUEST

Change ID: MM-CHANGE-003
Title: Upcoming / Prepared / Recurring Meetings
Artifact Status: PENDING PRODUCT APPROVAL
Routing: PRODUCT

==================================================
1. EXISTING APPROVED BEHAVIOR
==================================================

The approved PRODUCT-SPEC v1.0 supports meeting creation/configuration,
participant roster selection, explicit Start, live measurement, explicit
End, final receipt, and History for completed meetings.

The approved baseline does not define a persistent future/prepared
meeting state, rescheduling/deletion before Start, missed/not-held
prepared meetings, recurring indicators/duplication, or a PREPARED
lifecycle state.

PRODUCT-SPEC v1.0 remains unchanged by this CHANGE-REQUEST.

==================================================
2. PROPOSED NEW REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

MR-003-01  A meeting can be saved before Start as a persistent PREPARED
meeting.

MR-003-02  A PREPARED meeting does not measure time and does not
accumulate meeting cost.

MR-003-03  A PREPARED meeting can be edited before Start.

MR-003-04  A PREPARED meeting can be rescheduled before Start.

MR-003-05  Rescheduling updates the existing prepared meeting rather than
requiring creation of a new meeting record.

MR-003-06  The system records/indicates that a prepared meeting was
rescheduled, including a Last Updated or equivalent visible change
indicator.

MR-003-07  A PREPARED meeting can be deleted before Start.

MR-003-08  If the planned date/time passes without Start, the meeting can
appear in History as MISSED / NOT HELD.

MR-003-09  A MISSED / NOT HELD meeting can be retained in History, deleted,
or used as the basis for creating a Manual Entry for a meeting that was
actually held.

MR-003-10  A meeting may be marked with a RECURRING indicator/tag.

MR-003-11  A RECURRING meeting can be duplicated into another meeting to
make preparation of another occurrence easier.

MR-003-12  A duplicated recurring occurrence is an independent meeting
record and can be edited independently.

MR-003-13  Recurring behavior is based on an indicator/tag and duplication,
not an automatic recurrence/calendar scheduling engine.

MR-003-14  The prepared-meeting lifecycle is:
PREPARED -> LIVE -> ENDED.

MR-003-15  A prepared meeting that passes its planned date/time without
Start may enter MISSED / NOT HELD status for History purposes.

==================================================
3. PRODUCT RULES
==================================================

PR-003-01  PREPARED means the meeting is already created and saved with
its planned/configured information, but Start has not been pressed.

PR-003-02  PREPARED is a persistent product state, not merely unsaved
configuration and not a reusable template.

PR-003-03  PREPARED has no running timer and no accumulating cost.

PR-003-04  The normal live flow begins only when Start is explicitly used.

PR-003-05  Before Start, the organizer may edit, reschedule, or delete the
prepared meeting.

PR-003-06  Cancellation is not a separate product state required for this
capability.

PR-003-07  A missed prepared meeting is represented as MISSED / NOT HELD
rather than as a completed meeting with zero cost.

PR-003-08  A missed meeting may be retained, deleted, or used as the basis
for Manual Entry when the meeting actually occurred.

PR-003-09  Recurring is an organizational convenience: a tag/indicator plus
duplication behavior.

PR-003-10  There is no automatic recurring-series engine in this change.
Each duplicated occurrence is independent.

PR-003-11  No calendar event is automatically created or synchronized by
this change.

==================================================
4. OPEN QUESTIONS

None required for the core Product decision package at this stage.

UI-level details may still be resolved downstream, including the exact
presentation of MISSED / NOT HELD and the reschedule indicator.

PLD-003-02  Confirm that MISSED / NOT HELD is a History representation and

PLD-003-03  Confirm the recurring model as tag/indicator + duplication,

PLD-003-04  If approved, authorize incorporation into a new Product Spec

No approval is granted by this CHANGE-REQUEST.

==================================================
6. OUT OF SCOPE
==================================================

- Automatic calendar scheduling.
- Google Calendar, Outlook, Teams, Zoom, or other calendar/conferencing
  integration.
- Automatic invitations or participant notifications.
- Automatic recurring-series generation.
- Complex recurrence rules (weekly/monthly patterns, exceptions, RRULE,
  series editing, etc.).
- Automatic attendance detection.
- Separate cancellation workflow/state.
- Cross-user shared scheduling or organization-wide calendar management.

==================================================
7. DEPENDENCIES
==================================================

- Meeting entity and lifecycle/state model.
- Meeting creation and persistence.
- Participant roster and selection.
- Cost profiles / hourly rates.
- Start / live measurement / End.
- History and status representation.
- Date/time handling.
- Manual Entry change MM-CHANGE-002 for conversion of a missed meeting
  into reconstructed historical data.
- Any approved external-cost behavior from MM-CHANGE-004 must remain
  compatible with prepared, live, ended, and missed representations.

==================================================
8. DOWNSTREAM DESIGN IMPACT
==================================================

Design must introduce a visible PREPARED meeting state and distinguish it
from LIVE and ENDED meetings.

Design impact includes:
- save-before-Start flow;
- Prepared meeting list/detail;
- edit before Start;
- reschedule interaction;
- visible indication that a meeting was changed/rescheduled;
- delete before Start;
- transition from PREPARED to LIVE;
- MISSED / NOT HELD History presentation;
- conversion path from MISSED / NOT HELD to Manual Entry;
- RECURRING tag/indicator;
- duplicate-meeting action;
- independent editing of duplicated meetings.

Design must not imply that PREPARED meetings are running or accumulating
cost.

==================================================
9. DOWNSTREAM TECHNICAL IMPACT
==================================================

Technical definition must support:
- persisted PREPARED status/state;
- planned date/time;
- editing before Start;
- rescheduling with change indication/Last Updated;
- deletion before Start;
- transition PREPARED -> LIVE -> ENDED;
- detection/representation of planned meetings that pass without Start;
- MISSED / NOT HELD History status;
- conversion/reference path to Manual Entry;
- RECURRING indicator/tag;
- duplication of a meeting into an independent record;
- no timer/cost accumulation while PREPARED;
- no automatic calendar or recurrence engine.

Technical design must preserve the approved explicit Start requirement for
live measurement.
