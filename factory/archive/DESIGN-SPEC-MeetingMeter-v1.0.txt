1. PURPOSE
This DESIGN-SPEC defines the approved UX, information architecture, visual direction, components, interaction states, responsive behavior, accessibility requirements, and implementation-facing design constraints for MeetingMeter.

2. PRODUCT BASELINE
PRODUCT-SPEC v1.0 remains unchanged and is the canonical product requirements baseline. This DESIGN-SPEC does not introduce product functionality.

The approved baseline includes: account/login; personal profile (name, job title, hourly rate); internal people; Guests; known Guest rate; explicitly unknown Guest cost; meeting creation and roster configuration; individual selection; Select All; explicit Start; individual measurement; ACTIVE/PAUSED/RESUMED/LEFT; live elapsed time; individual and total estimated cost; explicit End; stable final result; receipt; private history; PDF/Image/CSV export; estimate framing; cool/light/mildly humorous tone.

3. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
Primary areas:
- Home
- People
- Meetings / History
- Profile
- Active Meeting

Core flow:
CONFIGURE -> START -> LIVE METER -> END -> FINAL RESULT / RECEIPT -> HISTORY

Participant lifecycle:
ACTIVE <-> PAUSED
ACTIVE -> LEFT
RESUME returns PAUSED to active measurement.
LEAVE ends measurement for that participant.

4. CORE SURFACES
HOME: primary entry, active/recent meeting context, navigation.
MEETING CONFIGURATION: meeting setup, roster, individual selection, Select All, Guest handling, explicit Start.
LIVE METER: meeting context, prominent real-time estimated total cost, elapsed time, participant manifest, participant states/actions, known cost and unknown-cost treatment, explicit End.
FINAL RESULT / RECEIPT: meeting, creator, date/time, duration, participants, measured participation times, relevant cost information, final estimated cost, estimate framing, PDF/Image/CSV export.
PEOPLE: organizer-managed internal people and Guests.
PROFILE: name, job title, hourly rate.
HISTORY / MEETINGS: creator-owned completed meetings and results, private access.

5. VISUAL DIRECTION
Cool, light, restrained, mildly humorous, high numerical clarity, clean hierarchy, low visual noise.

Primary hierarchy:
1) meeting context/state
2) estimated cost
3) elapsed time and participant state
4) primary actions
5) supporting metadata/actions

The estimated cost remains prominent and clearly framed as an estimate.

8. STATES
Global: default, hover, focus, active, selected, disabled, loading,
empty, error, success, validation.

Participant:
ACTIVE = measurement running.
PAUSED = measurement stopped, participant remains in meeting.
RESUMED = measurement continues.
LEFT = measurement ended for that participant.

Unknown Guest cost must be visibly distinct from zero.

Meeting:
CONFIGURED = not measuring.
STARTED/LIVE = measurement according to participant states.
ENDED = measurement stopped and final result stable.

9. RESPONSIVE
Desktop: persistent/collapsible navigation as appropriate; multi-column layouts where useful; live cost and participant manifest remain coherent.
Tablet: navigation may collapse; no horizontal scrolling; preserve cost/action hierarchy.
Mobile: single-column; compact navigation; prominent cost; actionable participant rows; stacked secondary information; accessible touch targets.

Approved Figma mobile baseline includes Live Meter and Home mobile contexts.

10. ACCESSIBILITY
Sufficient contrast; visible keyboard focus; semantic headings; accessible control labels; accessible status communication where relevant; states not conveyed by color alone; usable touch targets; dialog focus management; keyboard support; reduced-motion consideration.

11. TONE / ESTIMATE FRAMING
Cost is always presented as an estimate, not accounting/payroll/billing/payment. Humor is secondary to clarity and must not target individual participants.
