CHANGE-REQUEST

Change ID: MM-CHANGE-002
Title: Retroactive / Manual Entry
Artifact Status: PENDING PRODUCT APPROVAL
Routing: PRODUCT

==================================================
1. EXISTING APPROVED BEHAVIOR
==================================================

The approved PRODUCT-SPEC v1.0 defines the normal MeetingMeter flow as:

- meeting is created/configured;
- the organizer explicitly starts measurement;
- active participant time is measured;
- participants may be paused, resumed, or marked as having left;
- the organizer explicitly ends the meeting;
- a final estimated result is generated;
- the completed meeting is retained in History.

The approved baseline distinguishes overall meeting duration from each
participant's measured participation time.

The approved baseline does not define a retroactive/manual-entry flow,
manual-entry provenance, manually reconstructed participant duration,
or post-creation editing/recalculation rules for manual meetings.

PRODUCT-SPEC v1.0 remains unchanged by this CHANGE-REQUEST.

==================================================
2. PROPOSED NEW REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

MR-002-01  A user can create a meeting retrospectively as MANUAL ENTRY
when the meeting was genuinely held but live measurement was not used.

MR-002-02  Manual Entry requires a meeting date.

MR-002-03  Meeting time is optional and may be omitted when the exact
time is not known.

MR-002-04  Manual Entry requires a meeting duration.

MR-002-05  Manual Entry supports adding internal people and guests using
the existing participant model.

MR-002-06  For each participant, the user can enter individual measured /
reconstructed participation duration directly. Arrival/departure may be
used as an equivalent input method if supported by the final design, but
the canonical product value is individual participation duration.

MR-002-07  A participant may have zero minutes of participation. A zero-
duration participant remains a participant but contributes zero
participant cost.

MR-002-08  Individual participation duration must satisfy:
0 <= individual participation duration <= meeting duration.
Negative duration is invalid.

MR-002-09  Individual participation durations do not need to sum to the
meeting duration.

MR-002-10  Manual Entry uses the same applicable hourly-rate/cost-profile
rules as the normal meeting cost calculation.

MR-002-11  Manual Entry is explicitly identified with provenance:
MEASUREMENT TYPE: MANUAL ENTRY.

MR-002-12  A manual-entry reason may be entered by the user and is stored
with the meeting for History/detail visibility. The reason is not a
required controlled category list unless separately approved.

MR-002-13  Manual meetings can be edited after creation.

MR-002-14  Editing a manual meeting recalculates the affected result.

MR-002-15  Manual meetings do not fabricate Start/Pause/Resume/Leave
measurement events. They represent reconstructed meeting data.

MR-002-16  The receipt/result identifies the meeting as Manual Entry and
makes clear that the result was not produced by live measurement.

MR-002-17  Manual meetings are retained in History as meetings created
through Manual Entry.

==================================================
3. PRODUCT RULES
==================================================

PR-002-01  A manual meeting is a genuine historical meeting record, not
a live measurement session performed retroactively.

PR-002-02  Meeting duration and individual participation duration are
separate values.

PR-002-03  A participant who was expected to attend but did not attend
may be included with 0 minutes of participation.

PR-002-04  Zero participation is valid and produces zero participant
cost; zero must not be interpreted as unknown cost.

PR-002-05  Individual participation duration cannot be negative or exceed
meeting duration.

PR-002-06  Individual participation durations are independent and do not
have to add up to the meeting duration.

PR-002-07  Manual Entry must remain visibly distinguishable from live
measurement in History and the receipt/result.

PR-002-08  Editing a manual meeting changes the reconstructed data and
recalculates the estimated result.

PR-002-09  Manual Entry must not imply a false live event history.

PR-002-10  The existing estimated-cost framing and non-accounting,
non-payroll character of MeetingMeter remain applicable.

==================================================
4. OPEN QUESTIONS
==================================================

None required for the core Product decision package at this stage.

Implementation/design may still need to resolve UI-level details such as
whether duration is entered in minutes or through an hours/minutes control,
without changing the product rules above.

==================================================
6. OUT OF SCOPE
==================================================

- Fabricating or importing historical Start/Pause/Resume/Leave events.
- Automated reconstruction of attendance.
- Calendar integration for discovering past meetings.
- Payroll/accounting/billing/payment functionality.
- Automatic salary or hourly-rate inference.
- Manipulation of another user's private meeting history.
- Any change to the approved Product character or privacy model unless
  separately approved.

==================================================
7. DEPENDENCIES
==================================================

- Meeting entity and meeting history.
- Existing participant roster model.
- Internal and guest participant handling.
- Hourly rates / cost profiles.
- Existing participant cost calculation.
- Meeting duration and individual participation duration.
- Final result and receipt.
- History and export representation.
- Change to the approved final-result rules must be coordinated with
  any other approved/pending changes that affect post-completion editing.

==================================================
8. DOWNSTREAM DESIGN IMPACT
==================================================

Design must add a clear Manual Entry flow without confusing it with live
measurement.

Design impact includes:
- Manual Entry creation form;
- required date and optional time;
- meeting duration input;
- participant-level participation duration input;
- support for 0-minute participants;
- validation messaging for invalid duration values;
- manual-entry provenance indicator;
- optional manual-entry reason;
- History/detail presentation of Manual Entry;
- receipt/result presentation showing Manual Entry provenance;
- edit/recalculate experience for manual meetings.

Design must preserve the existing distinction between meeting duration
and individual measured participation time.

==================================================
9. DOWNSTREAM TECHNICAL IMPACT
==================================================

Technical definition must support:
- meeting provenance / measurement type MANUAL_ENTRY;
- persisted manual meeting date and optional time;
- meeting duration;
- persisted participant participation durations;
- zero-duration participants;
- validation that participation duration is within meeting duration;
- recalculation after manual edits;
- persisted manual-entry reason where supplied;
- History/receipt/export representation of manual provenance;
- separation between reconstructed manual data and live measurement events.

Technical implementation must not silently alter the approved live-meeting
state machine or create fake live measurement events.
