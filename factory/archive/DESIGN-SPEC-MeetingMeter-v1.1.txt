6. V1.1 MEETING STATES AND PRESENTATION
==================================================

6.1 PREPARED

A saved future meeting.

Design presentation:
- planned meeting information;
- clear PREPARED state;
- no elapsed-time accumulation;
- no accumulated cost;
- editable before Start;
- reschedulable before Start;
- deletable before Start;
- Start action.

6.2 LIVE

Active meeting with live participant measurement.

Retain the existing Live Meter hierarchy while applying the Dark
visual system.

At minimum, preserve clear presentation of:
- meeting context/state;
- estimated cost;
- elapsed time;
- participant states;
- primary actions;
- supporting metadata.

6.3 ENDED

Completed meeting.

Participant measurement is locked.

The current result remains ESTIMATED.

External-cost editing may occur after End as defined below.

6.4 MISSED / NOT HELD

A History representation for a PREPARED meeting whose planned
date/time passed without Start.

Design must distinguish it from ENDED and must not present it as a
completed zero-cost meeting.

Supported Product actions may include:
- retain;
- delete;
- use as basis for Manual Entry.

6.5 MANUAL ENTRY

MANUAL ENTRY is provenance/type for a reconstructed historical
meeting.

It is not a live measurement state.

The Design must not imply fabricated Start/Pause/Resume/Leave events.

6.6 RECURRING

RECURRING is a visible tag/indicator.

Duplication creates an independent meeting record.

The Design must not imply automatic recurrence or calendar
synchronization.

==================================================
7. MANUAL ENTRY
==================================================

7.1 CREATION

Provide a dedicated Manual Entry creation surface.

Required:
- date;
- meeting duration;
- participants;
- individual participation duration.

Optional:
- time;
- manual-entry reason.

7.2 PROVENANCE

MANUAL ENTRY must be visible as provenance on relevant creation,
result, receipt, and History surfaces.

7.3 VALIDATION

For each participant:
0 <= participation duration <= meeting duration

Zero duration is valid.

Negative duration is invalid.

Participation durations do not need to sum to meeting duration.

7.4 EDITING

Manual meetings can be edited.

Affected estimated results are recalculated.

The UI should clearly indicate that the record is reconstructed data.

7.5 RECEIPT / HISTORY

Receipt includes, where applicable:
- meeting;
- creator;
- date/time where known;
- duration;
- participants;
- reconstructed participation;
- relevant participant cost information;
- external cost items;
- participant total;
- external total;
- current total estimated meeting cost;
- MANUAL ENTRY provenance;
- manual-entry reason where applicable;
- Last Updated where applicable.

History identifies MANUAL ENTRY and displays the current result.

==================================================
8. PREPARED / MISSED / RECURRING
==================================================

8.1 PREPARED CREATION

Saving a PREPARED meeting must not start measurement.

8.2 PREPARED ACTIONS

- Edit
- Reschedule
- Delete
- Start

8.3 RESCHEDULING

Rescheduling updates the existing prepared meeting and shows a visible
Last Updated or equivalent change indication.

8.4 MISSED / NOT HELD

The History representation must:
- identify MISSED / NOT HELD;
- distinguish it from ENDED;
- avoid implying measured duration or zero cost;
- support the Product-defined follow-up actions.

8.5 RECURRING

Recurring presentation:
- visible RECURRING tag/indicator;
- duplication action;
- each duplicate visually treated as an independent meeting.

No recurrence engine or calendar synchronization is introduced.

==================================================
9. EXTERNAL COSTS
==================================================

9.1 EXTERNAL COST ITEMS

A meeting may contain zero or more external cost items.

Each item supports:
- description/name;
- amount;
- EUR (€) currency.

Multiple items are supported.

Zero is allowed.

Negative amounts are prohibited.

9.2 COST SEPARATION

Participant and external costs remain visually separate.

Display:

Participant Cost Total
External Cost Total
Total Estimated Meeting Cost

Calculation:

Total Estimated Meeting Cost =
Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total

9.3 COST LIBRARY

Cost Library provides reusable external cost items.

Each library item contains:
- name;
- default amount;
- EUR (€) currency.

Selecting an item copies its default amount into the meeting.

A meeting-specific override:
- changes the applied meeting amount only;
- does not change the library default;
- remains independent of later library-default changes.

9.4 POST-END EDITING

After End:
- participant measurement remains locked;
- external costs may be edited;
- editing does not restart measurement;
- editing does not change participant measurement history;
- current estimated total may change;
- Last Updated changes;
- Receipt/History reflect the current result.

==================================================
10. RECEIPT / HISTORY
==================================================

10.1 RECEIPT

Receipt remains an estimate-oriented result surface.

v1.1 extends it with:
- MANUAL ENTRY provenance where applicable;
- external cost items;
- participant total;
- external total;
- combined current total;
- Last Updated where applicable;
- relevant prepared/missed/recurring context where applicable.

10.2 HISTORY

History remains private and creator-owned.

v1.1 extends presentation with:
- MANUAL ENTRY;
- MISSED / NOT HELD;
- RECURRING;
- external cost summary;
- current Total Estimated Meeting Cost;
- Last Updated where applicable.

13. RESPONSIVE DESIGN
==================================================

Desktop:
- preserve the current Dark cockpit hierarchy;
- support multi-column layouts where useful;
- keep cost and state prominent;
- maintain clear separation of participant and external cost data.

Tablet:
- collapse navigation as needed;
- preserve meeting state and total visibility;
- stack or regroup dense cost information without horizontal scrolling.

Mobile:
- single-column;
- prominent total;
- compact but accessible participant rows;
- stacked external cost items;
- usable Manual Entry form;
- readable Cost Library selection;
- accessible Prepared actions;
- no horizontal scrolling.

Receipt and History must remain legible and usable on narrow screens.

==================================================
14. ACCESSIBILITY
==================================================

Dark theme:
- maintain sufficient contrast;
- provide visible focus;
- do not rely on color alone for state;
- provide accessible state labels;
- preserve semantic headings;
- provide accessible form labels and validation;
- maintain touch target sizes;
- manage dialog focus;
- support keyboard operation;
- support reduced motion.

Meeting states and provenance must be understandable without color.

Numerical results must remain clearly labeled as estimates.

==================================================
15. DESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS
==================================================

- Use the current Dark reference's high-density telemetry hierarchy as
  the visual language for MeetingMeter.
- Keep the total estimated cost as the dominant numerical result.
- Use distinct badges for PREPARED, MISSED / NOT HELD, MANUAL ENTRY,
  and RECURRING.
- Keep external costs visually separate from participant measurement.
- Use clear edit affordances for post-End external-cost changes.
- Preserve the current tactical/financial visual character without
  introducing unsupported Product capabilities.
- Keep humor subordinate to clarity.

These are recommendations, not Product requirements.
