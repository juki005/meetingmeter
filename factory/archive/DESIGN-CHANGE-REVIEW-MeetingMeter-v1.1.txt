3. GOVERNANCE DECISION — DARK RETAINED
==================================================

DECISION:

DARK / TACTICAL FINTECH is the intended canonical visual direction
for the proposed MeetingMeter DESIGN-SPEC v1.1.

The current Stitch/Figma visual artifact is retained as the visual
baseline/reference for this revised Design direction.

The previous Light visual token set is superseded as the intended
visual direction for v1.1.

The previous Light Design baseline is not deleted or rewritten.
DESIGN-SPEC v1.0 remains preserved as the historical approved Design
baseline.

RATIONALE:

- The current MeetingMeter implementation and Stitch-generated visual
  artifact already establish a coherent Dark / Tactical FinTech
  direction.
- QA-EXECUTION v1.1 verified conformance to the Stitch reference while
  documenting the discrepancy against the older Light Design tokens.
- PROJECT-STATE v1.8 identifies the Light-vs-Dark conflict as a Design
  governance issue rather than a Product requirement conflict.
- The Product v1.1 baseline does not require a Light visual theme and
  preserves the existing product tone, estimate framing, numerical
  clarity, and core behavior.
- The Product Lead has now explicitly selected retention of the current
  Dark direction as the intended Design direction for this revision.

GOVERNANCE CONSEQUENCE:

- The Dark direction is promoted into the proposed DESIGN-SPEC v1.1.
- The Light token set is explicitly superseded for the revised v1.1
  intended visual baseline.
- DESIGN-SPEC v1.0 remains preserved and is not altered.
- The current Stitch/Figma artifact remains the visual reference.
- No visual regeneration from the Light baseline is requested.
- No implementation code change is made by this Design review.
- The revised Design package remains DRAFT / PROPOSED until the formal
  DESIGN_APPROVAL gate.
- Technical progression remains governed by Factory state and requires
  Design approval before the revised Design baseline is treated as
  approved.

7. NEW V1.1 DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

7.1 MANUAL ENTRY

Provide a dedicated Manual Entry creation/edit experience.

Required inputs:
- date;
- meeting duration;
- participants;
- individual participation duration.

Optional inputs:
- time;
- manual-entry reason.

The interface must visibly identify the record as MANUAL ENTRY.

Validation must communicate:
- participation duration cannot be negative;
- participation duration cannot exceed meeting duration;
- zero participation duration is valid;
- individual durations do not need to sum to meeting duration.

Manual Entry must be visually distinct from a live measured meeting
without suggesting a different Product calculation model.

The UI must not fabricate Start, Pause, Resume, or Leave events.

Editing a Manual Entry must visibly communicate that the meeting is
being reconstructed/edited and that the affected estimated result is
recalculated.

History must identify MANUAL ENTRY.

Receipt must identify MANUAL ENTRY and include the relevant date/time
where known, duration, reconstructed participation, relevant
participant cost information, current estimated total, estimate
framing, and optional reason where applicable.

7.2 PREPARED

Provide a saved future-meeting presentation.

PREPARED must visually communicate:
- not started;
- no accumulated time;
- no accumulated meeting cost;
- planned meeting information where available.

Supported actions:
- Edit
- Reschedule
- Delete
- Start

Delete must be treated as a destructive action and use the retained
confirmation pattern.

Rescheduling must show a visible Last Updated or equivalent change
indication.

7.3 MISSED / NOT HELD

A PREPARED meeting whose planned date/time passes without Start may be
represented in History as MISSED / NOT HELD.

The visual treatment must distinguish MISSED / NOT HELD from:
- ENDED;
- LIVE;
- PREPARED;
- MANUAL ENTRY.

MISSED / NOT HELD must not look like a completed meeting with zero
cost.

The interface may expose the Product-supported actions to retain,
delete, or use the record as the basis for Manual Entry.

7.4 RECURRING

A recurring meeting receives a visible RECURRING tag/indicator.

Duplication is presented as a deliberate action that creates an
independent meeting record.

The Design must not imply an automatic recurrence engine or calendar
synchronization.

7.5 EXTERNAL COSTS

Provide an external-cost area within relevant meeting configuration,
live, result, receipt, and history contexts.

External cost items support:
- name/description;
- amount;
- EUR (€) currency;
- multiple items.

Validation:
- zero is valid;
- negative amounts are invalid.

Participant costs and external costs must remain visually separate.

The UI must show:

Participant Cost Total
+
External Cost Total
=
Total Estimated Meeting Cost

The combined total must remain clearly framed as estimated.

7.6 COST LIBRARY

Provide a Cost Library selection experience for reusable external
cost items.

A library item contains:
- name;
- default amount;
- EUR (€) currency.

Selecting a library item copies its default amount into the meeting.

A meeting-specific override must be visibly distinct from the library
default.

Changing the applied meeting price must not imply that the library
default was changed.

The applied meeting price must be represented as belonging to the
meeting independently of later library-default changes.

7.7 POST-END EXTERNAL-COST EDITING

After End, participant measurement is locked.

External-cost items remain editable as permitted by Product v1.1.

The UI must make clear that:
- participant measurement is not restarting;
- participant measurement history is not changing;
- the current estimated total may change;
- Last Updated changes after an external-cost edit.

The result remains ESTIMATED.

Receipt and History must reflect the current result and Last Updated
where applicable.

8. CHANGED DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

8.1 VISUAL SYSTEM

Changed from v1.0:
- intended visual direction changes from Light to Dark / Tactical
  FinTech;
- v1.0 Light tokens are superseded for the v1.1 intended baseline;
- current Stitch/Figma becomes the visual reference for the revised
  baseline.

Unchanged:
- numerical clarity;
- estimate framing;
- restrained information hierarchy;
- accessibility requirements;
- no color-only state communication.

8.2 FINAL RESULT STABILITY

The v1.0 Design treatment of a stable final result is retained for
participant measurement.

It is revised only to represent the approved Product v1.1 exception:
external-cost edits may change the current Total Estimated Meeting Cost
after End.

The UI must therefore distinguish:
- locked participant measurement;
- editable external cost;
- current estimated total;
- Last Updated.

8.3 HISTORY / RECEIPT

History and Receipt are extended to support:
- MANUAL ENTRY provenance;
- MISSED / NOT HELD;
- RECURRING indicator;
- external cost items;
- separate participant/external totals;
- current combined total;
- Last Updated where applicable.

==================================================
9. DESIGN RECOMMENDATIONS
==================================================

These are Design recommendations, not new Product requirements:

- Use the Dark visual hierarchy to make the estimated total the primary
  numerical focal point.
- Use emerald accent treatment consistently for primary positive/live
  telemetry while preserving non-color state indicators.
- Use distinct visual badges for PREPARED, LIVE, ENDED, MISSED /
  NOT HELD, MANUAL ENTRY, and RECURRING.
- Treat external costs as a visually separate ledger/category rather
  than merging them into participant rows.
- Make post-End external-cost editing visibly different from editing
  participant measurement.
- Preserve compact, high-density telemetry patterns from the current
  Stitch reference without promoting unsupported telemetry features
  into Product requirements.
- Keep humor subordinate to cost/result clarity.
- Maintain accessible focus, keyboard, contrast, and status semantics
  in the Dark theme.

14. RESPONSIVE / ACCESSIBILITY IMPACT
==================================================

Responsive:
- Manual Entry must remain usable on mobile without horizontal
  scrolling.
- Prepared actions must remain accessible on narrow screens.
- External cost lists must remain readable with multiple items.
- Cost Library selection must work in compact layouts.
- Receipt/result hierarchy must preserve total-cost prominence.
- Dark visual density must not reduce touch target usability.
- Tablet must preserve meeting state, total, and primary action
  hierarchy.

Accessibility:
- contrast must remain sufficient in the Dark theme;
- color cannot be the sole indicator of meeting state;
- state labels/badges require accessible text;
- keyboard focus must remain visible;
- dialogs must manage focus;
- validation must be announced clearly;
- touch targets remain usable;
- reduced-motion behavior remains supported;
- numerical result and estimate framing remain semantically clear.
