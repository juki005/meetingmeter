3. CHANGE 002 — RETROACTIVE / MANUAL ENTRY
==================================================

Classification:
FEATURE / MATERIAL PRODUCT CHANGE TO THE EXISTING MEETING MODEL

--------------------------------------------------
3.1 Existing approved behavior
--------------------------------------------------

The approved baseline covers live MeetingMeter measurement from explicit
Start through End. It does not define retrospective reconstruction.

--------------------------------------------------
3.2 Proposed new requirements
--------------------------------------------------

The proposed capability allows a genuinely held meeting to be entered
retroactively when live measurement was not used.

Core requirements:
- date required;
- time optional when known;
- meeting duration required;
- participants and applicable rates/cost profiles;
- individual participation duration per participant;
- zero-minute participants allowed;
- participation duration must be >= 0 and <= meeting duration;
- individual durations do not need to sum to meeting duration;
- manual provenance: MANUAL ENTRY;
- optional user-entered manual-entry reason stored with the meeting;
- manual meetings editable after creation;
- editing recalculates the estimated result;
- receipt/History identify Manual Entry;
- manual entries do not fabricate live measurement events.

--------------------------------------------------
3.3 Product rules
--------------------------------------------------

Manual Entry is a reconstructed historical record, not a retroactively
simulated live session. Meeting duration and participant participation
duration remain separate values. A person who did not attend can remain on
the participant list with zero participation.

--------------------------------------------------
3.4 Open questions
--------------------------------------------------

No core Product questions remain open. UI input details may be resolved
downstream.

--------------------------------------------------
3.5 Product Lead decisions required
--------------------------------------------------

Formal approval of the Manual Entry capability, validation rules,
provenance, editing/recalculation behavior, and History/receipt treatment.

--------------------------------------------------
3.6 Out of scope
--------------------------------------------------

No automated attendance reconstruction, calendar integration, fabricated
live event history, payroll/accounting/payment behavior, or rate inference.

--------------------------------------------------
3.7 Dependencies
--------------------------------------------------

Meeting entity, participant roster, rates, cost calculation, History,
receipt/export, and downstream handling of post-completion edits.

--------------------------------------------------
3.8 Downstream Design impact
--------------------------------------------------

New Manual Entry creation/edit flow, date/time/duration inputs,
participant-duration inputs, zero-duration handling, provenance indicator,
optional reason, History/detail treatment, receipt treatment, and
recalculation UI.

--------------------------------------------------
3.9 Downstream Technical impact
--------------------------------------------------

New meeting provenance, manual date/time fields, reconstructed participant
durations, validation, recalculation, reason persistence, and explicit
separation from live measurement events.

4. CHANGE 003 — UPCOMING / PREPARED / RECURRING
==================================================

Classification:
FEATURE / LIFECYCLE EXTENSION

--------------------------------------------------
4.1 Existing approved behavior
--------------------------------------------------

The approved baseline supports meeting configuration followed by explicit
Start, live measurement, End, receipt, and History. It does not define a
persistent future meeting state.

--------------------------------------------------
4.2 Proposed new requirements
--------------------------------------------------

The proposed capability introduces:
- persistent PREPARED meeting;
- no measurement or cost accumulation while PREPARED;
- edit before Start;
- reschedule before Start;
- visible indication of rescheduling/change;
- delete before Start;
- MISSED / NOT HELD History representation when planned time passes
  without Start;
- option to retain, delete, or use a missed meeting as the basis for
  Manual Entry;
- RECURRING tag/indicator;
- duplication of a recurring meeting into an independent meeting;
- independent editing of duplicated occurrences;
- lifecycle PREPARED -> LIVE -> ENDED, with MISSED / NOT HELD as the
  historical outcome for an unstarted planned meeting.

--------------------------------------------------
4.3 Product rules
--------------------------------------------------

PREPARED is a saved product state, not unsaved configuration and not a
reusable template. It has no timer and no cost accumulation. Cancellation
is not a separate required state. Recurring is intentionally a tag plus
duplication convenience, not a recurrence engine.

--------------------------------------------------
4.4 Open questions
--------------------------------------------------

No core Product questions remain open. Exact UI treatment of MISSED / NOT
HELD and reschedule indicators may be resolved downstream.

--------------------------------------------------
4.5 Product Lead decisions required
--------------------------------------------------

Formal approval of the PREPARED state, MISSED / NOT HELD treatment,
rescheduling/deletion behavior, and recurring-as-tag-plus-duplication model.

--------------------------------------------------
4.6 Out of scope
--------------------------------------------------

Calendar integration, automatic invitations, automatic recurrence engines,
complex recurrence rules, automatic attendance detection, and a separate
cancellation workflow.

--------------------------------------------------
4.7 Dependencies
--------------------------------------------------

Meeting lifecycle, persistence, roster, rates, History, date/time handling,
MM-CHANGE-002 for manual conversion, and compatibility with MM-CHANGE-004.

--------------------------------------------------
4.8 Downstream Design impact
--------------------------------------------------

Prepared state/list/detail, edit/reschedule/delete, change indicator,
MISSED / NOT HELD presentation, Manual Entry conversion, recurring tag,
and duplicate action.

--------------------------------------------------
4.9 Downstream Technical impact
--------------------------------------------------

Persistent PREPARED state, planned date/time, rescheduling metadata,
delete-before-start, lifecycle transitions, missed detection/representation,
recurring tag, independent duplication, and no cost accumulation while
prepared.

5. CHANGE 004 — EXTERNAL COSTS
==================================================

Classification:
SCOPE CHANGE / NEW CAPABILITY

Reason for material classification:
The proposed capability adds a new cost domain, a persistent Cost Library,
and permits external-cost mutation after finalization while live participant
measurement remains locked. This materially affects the approved final-result
stability rule and downstream model.

--------------------------------------------------
5.1 Existing approved behavior
--------------------------------------------------

The approved baseline calculates meeting cost from participant costs and
states that the final estimated result does not increase after End. It does
not define external costs or a Cost Library.

--------------------------------------------------
5.2 Proposed new requirements
--------------------------------------------------

The proposed capability introduces:
- zero or more external cost items;
- description + amount;
- zero allowed, negative prohibited;
- multiple items;
- separate participant and external totals;
- combined total = participant total + external total;
- editing external costs after finalization;
- recalculation after every edit;
- updated Receipt and History;
- Last Updated indication;
- persistent ESTIMATED framing;
- EUR (€) meeting currency;
- Cost Library with item name + default price;
- selection of a library item into a meeting;
- per-meeting price override;
- override does not modify the library default;
- meeting stores the applied price independently of later library changes.

--------------------------------------------------
5.3 Product rules
--------------------------------------------------

Live participant measurement remains locked after End. External costs may be
edited after End if this change is approved. Such edits do not restart the
meeting and do not alter participant measurement history; they only
recalculate the estimated total. The meeting currency is EUR (€).

--------------------------------------------------
5.4 Open questions
--------------------------------------------------

No core Product questions remain open. UI/library interaction details may
be resolved downstream.

--------------------------------------------------
5.5 Product Lead decisions required
--------------------------------------------------

Formal approval is specifically required for:
- external cost capability;
- post-finalization external-cost editing;
- recalculated final total;
- Last Updated indication;
- Cost Library and per-meeting override;
- EUR (€) currency rule.

--------------------------------------------------
5.6 Out of scope
--------------------------------------------------

Accounting/payroll/payment, negative costs, refunds/credits, currency
conversion, multi-currency meetings, third-party price lookup, OCR, required
categories, shared organization-wide libraries, and full immutable audit-log
infrastructure.

--------------------------------------------------
5.7 Dependencies
--------------------------------------------------

Meeting result, participant cost calculation, receipt, History, exports,
post-finalization editing, EUR representation, Cost Library, MM-CHANGE-002,
and MM-CHANGE-003.

--------------------------------------------------
5.8 Downstream Design impact
--------------------------------------------------

External Costs UI, item editing, validation, separate totals, combined total,
Cost Library selection, price override, Last Updated, updated receipt/history,
and persistent estimate framing.

--------------------------------------------------
5.9 Downstream Technical impact
--------------------------------------------------

External cost persistence, calculation, validation, post-finalization edits,
Last Updated metadata, receipt/history/export updates, Cost Library
persistence, selection/copy semantics, per-meeting override, and preservation
of applied historical price.

==================================================
6. COMBINED PRODUCT IMPACT
==================================================

Together, MM-CHANGE-002, MM-CHANGE-003, and MM-CHANGE-004 extend the
MeetingMeter model in three dimensions:

1. HISTORY / PROVENANCE
   Manual Entry allows a genuinely held meeting to be reconstructed when
   live measurement was unavailable.

2. LIFECYCLE / PLANNING
   Prepared meetings allow future meeting configuration before Start, with
   missed/not-held History handling and lightweight recurring duplication.

3. COST MODEL
   External costs extend the meeting total beyond participant time and add
   a reusable Cost Library. External costs may remain editable after End,
   while live participant measurement remains locked.

Combined conceptual model:

PREPARED
  -> LIVE
      -> ENDED

PREPARED
  -> MISSED / NOT HELD
      -> retain / delete / use as basis for MANUAL ENTRY

MANUAL ENTRY
  -> editable reconstructed meeting

LIVE / ENDED
  -> participant measurement remains locked after End
  -> external costs may be edited after End if MM-CHANGE-004 is approved

Combined total:
Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total
= Total Estimated Meeting Cost

Currency:
EUR (€)

7. CROSS-CHANGE DEPENDENCIES
==================================================

- MM-CHANGE-003 can hand a MISSED / NOT HELD meeting to MM-CHANGE-002 for
  reconstruction if the meeting actually occurred.
- MM-CHANGE-004 external costs can apply to live meetings and, if approved,
  to manual meetings.
- If prepared meetings support configuring external costs before Start,
  that behavior must be explicitly represented consistently in Design and
  Technical definitions.
- MM-CHANGE-004 introduces a deliberate exception to the existing
  final-result stability rule: participant measurement is locked, but the
  overall estimated result may change because external costs are edited.
- All three changes affect the Meeting entity, History, receipt/result, and
  downstream Design/Technical/QA validation.
