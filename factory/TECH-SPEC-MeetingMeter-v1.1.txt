4. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS REVISED BY PRODUCT v1.1
==================================================

4.1 MEETING STATE MODEL

v1.0:
CONFIGURED -> LIVE -> ENDED

v1.1:
PREPARED -> LIVE -> ENDED

Additional product/history representations:
- MISSED / NOT HELD
- MANUAL ENTRY provenance/type

PREPARED is a persistent saved future meeting.

PREPARED:
- does not measure;
- does not accumulate cost;
- can be edited before Start;
- can be rescheduled before Start;
- can be deleted before Start;
- can be started explicitly.

MISSED / NOT HELD is a History representation for a PREPARED meeting
whose planned date/time passed without Start.

MISSED / NOT HELD:
- does not accumulate cost;
- is not an ENDED zero-cost meeting;
- may be retained;
- may be deleted;
- may be used as the basis for Manual Entry.

MANUAL ENTRY is provenance/type, not a fabricated live measurement
state.

4.2 FINAL RESULT STABILITY

v1.0 absolute immutability is superseded.

After End:
- participant measurement is locked;
- participant measurement history cannot change;
- external cost items may be edited;
- current Total Estimated Meeting Cost may change;
- Receipt/History must reflect the current result;
- Last Updated must change;
- result remains ESTIMATED.

4.3 TOTAL COST

v1.0:
meeting cost = participant costs

v1.1:
Participant Cost Total =
sum of individual participant costs

External Cost Total =
sum of external cost items

Total Estimated Meeting Cost =
Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total

Participant and external totals remain technically separate.

5. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS INTRODUCED BY PRODUCT v1.1
==================================================

5.1 MANUAL ENTRY

A Manual Entry meeting requires:
- date;
- meeting duration;
- participants;
- individual participation duration.

Optional:
- time;
- manual-entry reason.

Validation:
0 <= individual participation duration <= meeting duration

Zero duration is valid.

Participation durations do not need to sum to meeting duration.

Manual Entry:
- has explicit MANUAL ENTRY provenance;
- reconstructs historical data;
- does not fabricate Start/Pause/Resume/Leave events;
- is editable;
- recalculates affected estimated results;
- appears in History and Receipt;
- may include the manual-entry reason.

5.2 PREPARED EDITING

PREPARED meetings require persistence before Start.

Required operations:
- create/save;
- edit;
- reschedule;
- delete;
- Start.

Rescheduling updates the existing prepared record and provides a visible
Last Updated or equivalent change indication.

No automatic recurrence engine is introduced.

5.3 MISSED / NOT HELD

The system must be able to represent a PREPARED meeting as MISSED /
NOT HELD when its planned date/time passes without Start.

This representation must be distinct from:
- ENDED;
- zero-cost completed meeting.

5.4 RECURRING

Recurring is:
- a tag/indicator;
- plus duplication.

Each duplicated occurrence is an independent meeting record.

No recurrence scheduler or calendar synchronization is introduced.

5.5 EXTERNAL COSTS

A meeting supports zero or more external cost items.

Each item contains:
- description/name;
- amount;
- EUR (€).

Rules:
- zero is valid;
- negative values are invalid;
- multiple items are valid.

Participant costs and external costs are separate.

5.6 COST LIBRARY

A user-level reusable Cost Library contains:
- name;
- default amount;
- EUR (€).

Selecting an item copies its default amount into the meeting.

A meeting-specific override:
- changes only the meeting's applied amount;
- does not change the library default;
- is stored independently;
- remains the applied historical price even if the library default
  later changes.

5.7 POST-END EXTERNAL COST EDITING

After End:
- external costs may be edited;
- participant measurement remains locked;
- meeting does not restart;
- participant measurement history is unchanged;
- current total is recalculated;
- Receipt/History reflect the current result;
- Last Updated changes;
- result remains ESTIMATED.

8. CALCULATION REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

LIVE PARTICIPANT COST

participant_cost =
hourly_rate * measured_seconds / 3600

PARTICIPANT COST TOTAL

Participant Cost Total =
sum of known participant costs

UNKNOWN PARTICIPANT COST

Unknown cost remains explicitly unknown and is not represented as
zero.

MANUAL PARTICIPATION

manual participant cost =
hourly_rate * manual_participation_duration / 3600

Zero duration:
- valid;
- participant remains represented;
- participant cost is zero.

EXTERNAL COST TOTAL

External Cost Total =
sum of external cost item amounts

COMBINED TOTAL

Total Estimated Meeting Cost =
Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total

EDITING

Manual Entry edits:
- recalculate affected participant/result values.

External-cost edits after End:
- recalculate External Cost Total;
- recalculate Total Estimated Meeting Cost;
- do not recalculate or alter locked participant measurement.

All current/final results remain ESTIMATED.

==================================================

OBSOLETE / REVISED: V1.0 `CONFIGURED` meeting state.
Replacement: PREPARED / LIVE / ENDED, with MISSED / NOT HELD history
representation and MANUAL ENTRY provenance.

OBSOLETE / REVISED: v1.0 statement that completed meeting final
calculated result cannot drift or change.
Replacement: participant measurement is locked after End, while
external-cost edits may change the current estimated total.

Replacement: participant total + external total.

OBSOLETE / REVISED: v1.0 data model has no persistent future meeting
state.
Replacement: PREPARED requires persistent planned configuration.

OBSOLETE / REVISED: v1.0 historical model only assumes completed live
meetings.
Replacement: History can include ENDED, MANUAL ENTRY, and MISSED / NOT
HELD.

OBSOLETE / REVISED: v1.0 export model is only a completed-meeting
export.
Replacement: receipt/export must represent Manual Entry and current
post-End external-cost result where applicable.

OBSOLETE / REVISED: v1.0 statement that any correction workflow after
End is outside MVP.

OBSOLETE / REVISED: v1.0 technical model has no Cost Library.

16. TECHNICAL RISKS
==================================================

R-01 State complexity
PREPARED, LIVE, ENDED, MISSED / NOT HELD, and MANUAL ENTRY provenance
increase lifecycle complexity.

Mitigation:
explicit state/provenance model and transition tests.

R-02 Historical data ambiguity
Manual Entry must not be mistaken for live measurement.

Mitigation:
explicit provenance and separate reconstructed data semantics.

R-03 Post-End mutation
External costs can change after End while participant measurement
cannot.

Mitigation:
separate participant measurement persistence from mutable external
cost data and recalculate only permitted totals.

R-04 Cost inconsistency
Participant and external totals could diverge.

Mitigation:
centralized deterministic calculation service and persisted applied
external amounts.

R-05 Cost Library historical drift
Library defaults could incorrectly rewrite existing meetings.

Mitigation:
copy/override persistence on meeting records.

R-06 Prepared scheduling semantics
Automatic recurrence/calendar behavior could be accidentally added.

Mitigation:
explicitly limit recurring to tag + duplication.

R-07 Unknown cost semantics
Unknown participant cost could accidentally become zero.

Mitigation:
explicit nullable/known state throughout model/API/UI/export.

R-08 Design mismatch
Implementation may diverge from the approved Dark/Stitch baseline.

Mitigation:
mandatory Antigravity Stitch MCP verification gate.

R-09 Export divergence
Receipt/export could omit new v1.1 totals/provenance.

Mitigation:
export from the canonical result model and verify against v1.1
acceptance criteria.

R-10 Change-history overreach
Technical implementation could accidentally introduce a full audit
system.

Mitigation:
support required Last Updated/change indication only unless a later
approved requirement explicitly adds audit logging.
