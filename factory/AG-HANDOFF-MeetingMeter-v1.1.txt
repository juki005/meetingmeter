==================================================
7. STATE INVARIANTS
==================================================

1. PREPARED does not measure.
2. Only explicit Start begins live measurement.
3. PREPARED can be edited/rescheduled/deleted before Start.
4. PREPARED -> LIVE is the live start transition.
5. LIVE -> ENDED stops participant measurement.
6. Participant measurement cannot restart after End.
7. MISSED / NOT HELD has no measured cost.
8. MANUAL ENTRY is provenance, not fabricated live state.
9. Manual participation cannot be negative or exceed meeting duration.
10. Zero manual participation is valid.
11. Recurring duplication creates an independent meeting.
12. External costs may be edited after End.
13. External edits never modify participant measurement.
14. External edits never transition ENDED -> LIVE.
15. Total = Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total.
16. Unknown participant cost is not zero.
17. All current/final results are ESTIMATED.

==================================================
9. CALCULATION AUTHORITY
==================================================

Participant:
hourly_rate * measured_seconds / 3600

Manual:
hourly_rate * reconstructed_participation_seconds / 3600

Participant Cost Total:
sum known participant costs

External Cost Total:
sum external cost items

Total Estimated Meeting Cost:
Participant Cost Total + External Cost Total

Unknown participant cost:
explicitly unknown, not zero.

Centralize this calculation to avoid UI/API/export divergence.
